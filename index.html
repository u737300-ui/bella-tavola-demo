One family, one table, twenty-five years

Bella Tavola began in 1998 when the Moretti family turned a small corner kitchen into the restaurant they had dreamed about back in Bologna. The menu has grown, the dining room has doubled — the rules haven't changed.

Pasta is rolled by hand every morning. The wood oven never cools before the last guest leaves. And nobody rushes you out of your chair: in our house, dinner takes as long as dinner takes.
